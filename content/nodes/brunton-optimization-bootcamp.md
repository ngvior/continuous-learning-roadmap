Steve Brunton's optimization series, begun in July 2026 with a companion book: the anatomy of an optimization problem, convexity (sets, functions, norms, Jensen's inequality, why local minima are global for convex problems), convex versus non-convex activation functions, and a 14-video gradient block covering gradient descent methods. It explains the training loop an AI engineer actually tunes: learning rates, optimizers and loss landscapes, which is why it is a Prerequisite of the P10 Fine-Tuning Pipeline. Prerequisites are partial derivatives and gradients (from the Calculus Node) and the applied linear algebra of the SVD playlist. Re-check playlist completeness before starting: as of 2026-09-10 the playlist had 44 entries of which 15 were private, unreleased videos, and the public gradient videos still carried placeholder titles; the 7-hour estimate covers only the public part.
