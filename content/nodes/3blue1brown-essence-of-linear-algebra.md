Grant Sanderson's 16-chapter visual introduction to linear algebra (3 hours of video): vectors, linear combinations and span, matrices as transformations, determinants, inverse matrices and rank, dot and cross products, change of basis, eigenvectors and eigenvalues. The owner is a linear-algebra beginner and this is the cheapest intuition build available; the hour estimate adds numpy practice (array shapes, dot products, matrix multiplication, broadcasting) so the ideas stick before they are needed. It is a Prerequisite of the Brunton SVD playlist, the Calculus and Neural Networks Node and Karpathy's course.
