Steve Brunton's 35-video statistics course (9.9 hours): sampling, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing and p-hacking, parameter estimation with bootstrapping and Monte Carlo, maximum likelihood and MAP estimation, Bayesian inference and conjugate priors, Gaussian mixture models and Bayesian linear regression, three of them with Python examples. It is the most AI-engineering-relevant statistics Resource on the channel: MLE and MAP are what cross-entropy and regularized losses are, and hypothesis testing and bootstrapping are what it takes to judge whether an eval result or an A/B change is real. It is a Prerequisite of the P13 Eval Dataset Generator for exactly that reason.
