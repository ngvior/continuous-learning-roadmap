The NLP section of Microsoft's ai-for-beginners course, scoped to six lessons: 13 Text Representation (bag of words and TF-IDF, the sparse-retrieval ancestor of BM25), 14 Semantic Word Embeddings (Word2Vec and GloVe, cosine similarity), 15 Language Modeling (training your own embeddings), 18 Transformers and BERT (a first Hugging Face fine-tune), 19 Named Entity Recognition, and 20 Large Language Models, Prompt Programming and Few-Shot Tasks. Each lesson pairs a README with a PyTorch notebook; do the PyTorch track and skip the labs unless time allows. This block supplies the mental model that the P6 RAG pipeline takes for granted: what a sparse vector, a dense embedding and an encoder model actually are. The rest of the course (symbolic AI, computer vision, other techniques) is out of scope for this Node.
