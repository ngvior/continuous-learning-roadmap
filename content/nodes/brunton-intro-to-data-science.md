Steve Brunton's 20-video whiteboard series (2.7 hours) on what data science and machine learning are: supervised versus unsupervised learning, cross-validation and overfitting, neural network architectures as a survey, digital twins, and four videos on data visualization hygiene. No code and no mathematics beyond notation, so it has no prerequisites. It is a vocabulary and mental-model Resource, placed first on the Foundations Lane so the Probability and Statistics playlists that follow have shared terms to build on.
