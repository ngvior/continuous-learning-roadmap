Andrej Karpathy's code-along lecture series, the anchor of the Machine Learning Lane: micrograd (backpropagation from scratch), the makemore series (bigram, MLP, activations and batch normalization, manual backprop, WaveNet-style hierarchical model), building GPT from scratch, and the tokenizer lecture. Every lecture is a live Jupyter build, so the estimate is working time (about 19 hours of video, 40 to 50 hours with the coding along) rather than watch time. Lecture 8, State of GPT, is a 43-minute talk rather than a lecture. Lecture 10, the 4-hour GPT-2 reproduction that needs a rented GPU, is not part of this Node; it is Attached Reading of the P10 Fine-Tuning Pipeline, where the GPU is rented anyway. This Node is the PyTorch on-ramp that P10 requires.
