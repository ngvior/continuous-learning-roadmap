A curated subset of Sriniously's concept-first, language-agnostic backend series, keeping only the videos that go beyond four years of NestJS, PostgreSQL and AWS work: 13 Caching (caching taxonomy, Redis internals), 14 Task queues and background jobs, 16 Error handling and fault-tolerant systems, 21.1 and 21.2 Backend scaling and performance engineering (latency percentiles, profiling, connection pooling, cache invalidation, load balancing, replicas and sharding, async processing), 22 Concurrency and parallelism (event loop versus threads, race conditions, locks), and 25 Real-time backends (SSE and WebSocket internals, pub/sub across machines, fan-out). About 13 hours; the other 24 videos in the playlist restate daily work and are out of scope. It is the conceptual Prerequisite of the P7 Semantic Cache, whose caching, queueing and load-testing decisions this material informs.
