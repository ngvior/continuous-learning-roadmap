Steve Brunton's 44-video probability course (10.6 hours): counting and combinatorics, conditional probability and Bayes' theorem, random variables, the common discrete and continuous distributions, expectation and variance, the law of large numbers, the central limit theorem, covariance and correlation. It requires only high-school algebra, with single-variable integrals from the continuous-distribution videos onward. This closes the owner's stated probability gap directly: Bayes, expectation, variance and the normal distribution are the minimum needed to read a loss function, a softmax output or an eval metric with understanding, and the playlist is the stated prerequisite of the Statistics course.
