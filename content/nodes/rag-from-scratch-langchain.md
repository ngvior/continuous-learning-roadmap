LangChain's short video series on retrieval-augmented generation, about 1.4 hours of watching plus the companion notebooks: indexing, retrieval, query translation (multi-query, RAG fusion, decomposition, step-back, HyDE), routing, query structuring, multi-representation indexing, RAPTOR and ColBERT. It is the most compact map of the RAG design space on the Roadmap and the direct preparation for the P6 RAG Pipeline with Hybrid Search. One video in the playlist is hidden or unavailable.
