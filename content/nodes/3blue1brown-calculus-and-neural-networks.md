Two 3Blue1Brown playlists, trimmed. From Essence of Calculus only chapters 1 to 4 and 8 (the essence of calculus, the paradox of the derivative, derivative formulas through geometry, the chain rule and product rule, integration and the fundamental theorem), about 1.5 hours; the chapters on Euler's number, L'Hopital and Taylor series are not needed for backpropagation intuition. Then the full Neural Networks playlist (3.6 hours): gradient descent and backpropagation in chapters 1 to 4, and LLMs, transformers and attention in chapters 5 to 7. It is the prelude to Karpathy's Zero to Hero: chapters 1 to 4 before his first lecture, 5 to 7 before his GPT lecture. It also supplies the gradient intuition the Brunton Optimization Bootcamp assumes.
