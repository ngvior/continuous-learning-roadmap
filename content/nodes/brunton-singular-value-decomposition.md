Steve Brunton's 43-video series (6.8 hours) following chapter 1 of Data-Driven Science and Engineering: the SVD and matrix approximation, dominant correlations, image compression, linear systems and least squares, the pseudoinverse, linear regression, PCA and eigenfaces, optimal truncation and randomized SVD, most of it with paired Python walkthroughs. It is applied linear algebra rather than an introduction, hence the 3Blue1Brown Prerequisite. It gives the concrete "why" for matrices: dimensionality reduction for embedding visualization, least squares as the ancestor of every regression, and low-rank approximation as the idea behind LoRA, which is why it is a Prerequisite of the P10 Fine-Tuning Pipeline.
